Can I donate?

Most blood banks in Bangladesh ask that you:

  • are 18 to 60 years old
  • weigh at least 50 kg (some accept 45 kg)
  • waited about 4 months since your last whole-blood donation
  • feel well, with no fever, cold or recent infection

The blood bank always does its own health check before you donate. Read the full guide.
