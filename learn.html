Eligibility

Who can donate blood?

Age, weight and health basics most blood banks check.

Read more

Most blood banks in Bangladesh accept donors who:

  • are 18 to 60 years old
  • weigh at least 50 kg (some accept 45 kg)
  • have normal blood pressure and haemoglobin on the day
  • feel well, with no fever, cold or infection

You may need to wait if you recently had a tattoo, surgery, a vaccine or certain medicines. Tell the staff honestly; it protects you and the patient.

Timing

How often can I donate?

Why BloodMate counts down about four months.

Read more

After a whole-blood donation your body replaces the fluid within a day, but red cells and iron take weeks to rebuild.

Most blood banks in Bangladesh ask for a gap of about 4 months between whole-blood donations. Your dashboard uses 120 days and shows the exact date you can give again.

Before

Getting ready to donate

Small things that make donating easier.

Read more
  • Sleep well the night before.
  • Eat a normal meal 2 to 3 hours before. Don't donate on an empty stomach.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Bring a photo ID and wear a shirt with loose sleeves.
After

Taking care after donating

What to do in the next 24 hours.

Read more
  • Rest for 10 to 15 minutes and have the snack and drink offered.
  • Drink extra fluids for the rest of the day.
  • Avoid heavy exercise and lifting with that arm until the next day.
  • If you feel dizzy, sit or lie down with your feet raised.

Then log the donation on your dashboard so your next eligible date updates.

Basics

Blood groups explained

ABO and Rh, and why O- and AB+ are special.

Read more

Your group is a letter (A, B, AB or O) plus Rh positive or negative. Giving the wrong group can cause a serious reaction, so hospitals always cross-match.

  • O- red cells can go to any group, so O- donors are often called universal donors.
  • AB+ patients can receive red cells from any group.
  • Negative groups are rarer in Bangladesh, so negative donors are especially valuable.

Myths

Common myths

Things people worry about that are not true.

Read more
  • "Donating makes you weak." Healthy adults usually feel normal within a day.
  • "It hurts a lot." You feel a short pinch from the needle; the donation takes about 10 minutes.
  • "You can catch an infection." Blood banks use a new sterile needle for every donor.
