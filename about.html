About

Why BloodMate exists

When someone needs blood in Bangladesh, families still post on Facebook and call everyone they know. BloodMate makes finding a matching, eligible donor nearby quick and calm.

What you can do here

1
Find donors

Search by blood group and district, including every compatible group.

2
Post emergency requests

Share the hospital, bags needed and deadline so donors can respond.

3
Manage your donor profile

Set availability, log donations and see when you can give again.

Project status

A complete front end, with a demo data layer

Every page and feature works, but there is no server yet. Profiles, requests and donations are saved in your own browser (localStorage), so other people can't see what you enter, and the sample donors and requests are not real people.

All storage goes through one small module, so connecting a real database later doesn't require rewriting the pages. The code is open on GitHub.
